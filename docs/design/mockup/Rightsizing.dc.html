How a safe resize works

  1. You confirm the new size; the VM must not be locked or mid-operation.
  2. Stops the VM, changes its size, starts it again.
  3. If it isn't healthy within 10 minutes, it goes back to the old size and starts.

Suggestions use 14 days of CPU and memory. Savings count only once a resize has finished.
